## Currency refinement basis

[Unicode CLDR supplemental currency data](https://unicode.org/reports/tr35/tr35-numbers.html#Supplemental_Currency_Data)
describes territory/currency associations, effective dates and currency-specific
fraction/rounding metadata. It is not an exchange-rate feed. The proposed bounded
country mapping and separate versioned rates are design choices for this application.
The proposal interprets “region changes” as selecting a different market for a new
quote/order or changing the store default, never rewriting the existing catalog's base currency.
Manually maintained rates provide deterministic assignment behavior; market accuracy
and a live rate provider would require a separate policy and integration.
